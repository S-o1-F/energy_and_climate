# RAG System for Energy and Climate Change

This project is a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system that helps users quickly find energy and climate data in long policy documents, such as National Energy and Climate Plans (NECPs), EU reporting guidelines and national rulebooks. Instead of searching through hundreds of pages by hand, users ask a question and get an answer with the source page, which makes it easier to fill in national reporting templates. The main target country is Serbia, and Romania's NECP and filled reporting annexes are used to test and evaluate the system. Documents are extracted, split into chunks, embedded and stored in PostgreSQL with pgvector, and answers are generated by a local LLM through Ollama.
